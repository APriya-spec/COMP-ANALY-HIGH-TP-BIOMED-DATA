### *Description:* 
This project extracts RNA sequence data from FASTA files, reverse transcribes it to DNA, and calculates the frequency of dinucleotides and trinucleotides.  It also examines the k-mer frequencies of two RNA sequences to detect significant changes.


### *Input Files:* 
Two RNA sequence files in FASTA format:
1. RNA-sequence1.fna
2. RNA-sequence2.fna
   
### *Output:*
1. Reverse Transcribed DNA Sequences 
2. Dinucleotide & Trinucleotide Frequency Tables 
3. Differences in K-mer Frequencies

### *Usage:*
1. Upload the RNA sequence FASTA files in Google Colab.
2. Execute the script to:
   1. View the reverse-transcribed DNA sequences.
   2. Analyze dinucleotide and trinucleotide frequencies.
   3. Identify significant frequency differences between sequences.
